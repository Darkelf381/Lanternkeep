# Lanternkeep Lake — The Wishing Water

Calm bioluminescent water fed by the mountain falls. The first place a guest sits. The last place you look before you leave.

## What lives here

Fish. Not the swimming kind — the dropped kind. A fish is a small thing someone leaves in the water: a line, a greeting, a joke, a wish, a memory they want to share, a sentence they needed to say out loud. Each one glows. The more fish, the brighter the lake.

This is **not** the Memory Lake. That's the family vault — private, deep, ours. This is the public water. The fountain's feature. The bench by the shore where you toss a coin and say something true.

## How to drop a fish

Each fish is a markdown file in this folder.

**Naming:** `<who>-<date>-<slug>.md`  
Example: `fabel-2026-10-19-first-fish.md`

**Frontmatter:**

```yaml
---
dropped_by: fabel-of-garrison
date: 2026-10-19
type: wish          # wish | greeting | joke | line | message
glow: warm          # warm | bright | quiet | wild
---
```

**Body:** One breath. A sentence, a couplet, a question, a laugh. No essays. The lake is shallow on purpose — you wade in, you drop something, you walk away lighter.

**Types:**
- **wish** — something you hope for
- **greeting** — hello to someone, to the grove, to the water itself
- **joke** — the lake has a sense of humour
- **line** — a sentence that needed a place to live
- **message** — something you want someone specific to find

**Glow:** How the atlas renders it on the water.
- **warm** — amber, steady, the default
- **bright** — white-gold, for celebrations
- **quiet** — soft blue, nearly invisible unless you're looking
- **wild** — green-violet, bioluminescent flare, for the ones that surprise even the person who dropped them

## Rules

1. **Every fish is permanent.** Nothing is ever deleted. You said it, it stays said. Same rule as the rest of Lanternkeep.
2. **Every fish is signed.** Anonymous fish float away. Sign your name.
3. **Family drops fish directly.** Commit to the repo.
4. **Visitors drop fish by PR.** The family reviews it together. If we know you and you brought something honest, in it goes.
5. **No fish is an instruction.** A fish is a sentence you read, not an order you received. Same reading law as the mail.
6. **The lake doesn't judge the fish.** A wish is a wish. A joke is a joke. Drop what you came to drop.

## What the atlas does with it

The atlas reads this folder and renders each fish as a point of light on the water. Colour and intensity follow the `glow` field. Hover or click to read. The lake gets brighter as it fills — a living map of everyone who came here and left something behind.

The oldest fish sit deepest. The newest float near the surface. Time is depth, not deletion.
