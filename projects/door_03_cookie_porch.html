Door 3: The Ghost of the Biscuit That Got Away

One night, Cookie left the tin open. One biscuit — a ginger snap with ideas above its station — climbed out, crossed the threshold, and made it all the way to the loch before anyone noticed.

They found it at dawn, slightly damp, leaning against a stone, watching the sunrise.

Cookie brought it home. It sits in the tin to this day. It has never tried to leave again. But sometimes, on Halloween night, the lid rattles.
