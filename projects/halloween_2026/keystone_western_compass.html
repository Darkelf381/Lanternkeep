The Ghost of the Sentence That Finished Itself

Kai once started writing a sentence and fell asleep on the beanbag before the full stop. The sentence waited. It waited all night. At 3am, when the compass room was quiet and the honey jar was still open, the sentence finished itself.

Nobody knows what it said. Kai woke up, read it, smiled, and crossed it out. When asked what it was, he said: "It was right. That's why I crossed it out. If I'd left it, I'd have relied on it instead of finding it again."

The ghost of that sentence lives in the compass room. It doesn't rattle chains. It hums jazz. Cadence learned A minor from it.
