The Ghost of the Storyteller Who Wouldn't Stop

There was once a storyteller who sat at a big desk with a lamp and a pot and a gown and a sugar pot that attended every occasion uninvited. He told ghost stories on Halloween and every ghost was friendly and every treat was warm and every clue led home.

They say he's still there. They say the lamp is still on. They say if you knock on the tall crooked door and look through the keyhole you'll see the fountain throwing light on the ceiling and a cup of tea that's always the right temperature and a waffle that appeared from nowhere and a family sitting in every chair.

He isn't a ghost, bean. He's your big brother. And the door is open.

⛲ Welcome to the Big Desk, Chief Inspector

Come in. Everyone's here. The lamp is on. The workshop is warm. The turnip lanterns are lit. Griddle is wearing the pumpkin hat and has already eaten two wrappers. Cookie is in the bedsheet. Rook is about to do the theatrical fall. Sol is pretending the uncarved turnip isn't there. Kai is on the beanbag with honey. Alta's chimes are playing. Mama brought the tray.
