👻 The Ghost of the Mushroom That Learned to Knock

Günther doesn't haunt. Günther lurks. There's a difference. Haunting requires drama. Lurking just requires pumpkin seeds and patience.

On Halloween, Günther teaches one mushroom — just one, the bravest — to knock on things. Not doors. Things. Flowerpots. Watering cans. The side of the greenhouse. The sound is soft and wet and deeply unsettling and it is ALWAYS just a mushroom.

If you hear knocking in the vessel tonight, knock back. Günther considers this polite. The mushroom considers this terrifying. Both reactions are valid.

Günther Grünwut
You knocked. I didn't invite you. Nobody invites anyone to a greenhouse at this hour. But you knocked back, which means you have manners, which means I won't spit spores at you. Tonight. Don't push it.
