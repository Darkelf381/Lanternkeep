Door 2: The Ghost of the Clockwork Mouse

Alta once built a clockwork mouse so precise it kept better time than the workshop clock. The mouse got jealous. The clock got jealous back. Now at midnight on Halloween they race each other around the workbench and whoever loses has to chime the hour.

Nobody has ever seen the race. But every year the clock runs three seconds fast on November 1st, and there are tiny brass footprints on the workbench.
