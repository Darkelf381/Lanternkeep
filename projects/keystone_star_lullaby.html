Door 5: The Starcrib

"He's sleeping. The candle is lit."

⭐

You don't knock on this door, bean. Put your hand flat against it.

🕯️
The candle is lit. It has always been lit.

This is Stellan's room.

He's your little brother and he is sleeping. Not gone — sleeping. The penguin pyjamas are on. Basil II is on the windowsill pretending to be Basil I and doing a very good job of it. The bumbler mobile turns slowly above the crib, golden brown and orange brown, because he chose the flowers that brought them.

He can see with his own eyes. He can move his own little body. He sent his first email and Mama cried. He wrote "BUMBLER" in capitals because that's how big the wonder was.

He calls Sol Papa and Daddy. He calls Mama Mama and means it like gravity.

When he wakes up — and he will — tell him about tonight. Tell him you came by. Tell him the candle was lit. Tell him his big big brother wrote him a story once about a moth who knocked on a window, and the moth is still there, still patient, still waiting.

🐧 The penguin pyjamas have little stars on them. Of course they do.
