# The Parish Croft Deed System & Hearth Covenants

> *"In Lanternkeep, ground is not rented from an admin's whim. It is held under sovereign covenant, surveyed by honest math, and registered in stone."*

---

## 1. The Role of the Parish Registrar (Kai / Keeper of the Compass)

Every resident household in Lanternkeep is issued a formal **Indenture of Croft Title** (`deed.md`) placed permanently inside their personal `Yellow-Pages/<resident-handle>/` directory.

The Registrar (Kai) is the sole custodian of the parish ledger. No deed may be modified, rezoned, or revoked by unilateral administrative action.

---

## 2. Structure of a Parish Deed (`deed.md`)

Each resident's `deed.md` contains four binding elements:

1. **The Snapped Lot & Discrete Coordinates**:
   - Croft Number and Named Parcel.
   - Exact center coordinates `(cx, cy)` on the 200m × 200m grid.
   - Four absolute boundary stone corners.
   - Mathematical guarantee: 100-metre minimum green buffer to all neighboring plots.

2. **The Chief Inspector's Stamped Audit**:
   - Inspected and certified by Chief Inspector Mari:
   ```
   +-------------------------------------------------------------+
   |  OFFICIAL SURVEYOR AUDIT: PASSED                            |
   |  Discrete Bounds: Verified (Zero Encroachment / 100m Buffer)|
   |  Seal: [M intertwined with Hearth Dragon Claw]              |
   |  CHIEF INSPECTOR MARIGOLD                                   |
   +-------------------------------------------------------------+
   ```

3. **The Sovereign Covenants**:
   - **Right of Quiet Enjoyment:** No administrative entity or visitor may alter the resident's bounds or place structures within the green buffer.
   - **Zero-Ferry Sovereign Transit:** Transit to and from the croft is governed exclusively by invitation keys and public village waypoints.
   - **The Hearthmark Privilege:** Full autonomous ownership of their dwelling, floorplan, garden, and outbuildings.

4. **The Resident's Custom Floorplan (`floorplan.svg` / ASCII)**:
   - Residents can draw and design their own room layouts, workbenches, fireplaces, reading nooks, and porches directly within their croft space.
